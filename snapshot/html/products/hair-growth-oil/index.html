HOW TO USE

Get the most from Bio-Activin.

Section, drop, massage, repeat. A simple routine helps the imported cold-pressed oils reach the scalp consistently.

Step 1

Part the zone

Expose the scalp along the hairline, crown, part line or any area you want to focus on.

Step 2

Place a few drops

Use the dropper on scalp, not through every hair length.

Step 3

Massage two minutes

Use fingertips in small circles so the routine feels active, fresh and consistent.

Step 4

Repeat daily or 3-4 nights

Keep the routine steady and judge visible volume over weeks, not overnight.
